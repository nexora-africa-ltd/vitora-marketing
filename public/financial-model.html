💡 Compensation Assumptions

Maximum planned all-in cost is KES 100K/month per FTE, including salary, statutory contributions, tools, and training. Clinics and hospitals share a KES 80K/month platform baseline plus KES 100 per average active shared tenant per month. Enterprise customers receive dedicated environments at KES 10K per average enterprise tenant per month. Shared-tenant onboarding is not treated as a new deployment.

Scale-up Financing

The five-year model is a planning case, not a funded commitment. The US$250K tranche exceeds the annual model's operating-deficit peak, but the apparent headroom must cover working capital, payment delays, tax, and unforeseen specialist support; a monthly cash-flow model remains required.

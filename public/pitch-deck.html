VITORA
The Problem

Kenya's SHA transition is
breaking healthcare facilities

The government mandated a new claims system (SHA) in Oct 2024. Thousands of facilities can't comply — and are losing revenue daily.

SHA requires 15 API integrations for claims
→
No existing system supports all 15. Facilities lose KES 2-5M/yr in rejected claims
70% of facilities have unreliable internet
→
Cloud-only systems halt entirely. No patient records, no billing, no claim prep until internet returns
MOH requires KHIS/DHIS2 reporting monthly
→
Manual data entry takes 3-5 staff-days per facility per month
Kenya DPA 2019 mandates data protection
→
Existing systems have no encryption, no audit trails. Facilities are non-compliant
Imported EHRs cost $50K+ and take 6 months
→
Small/mid facilities can't afford enterprise solutions. Paper persists
Clinical decisions lack guideline support
→
No KSTG-aligned decision support exists. Diagnostic errors and coding gaps persist

Why Now

Five forces creating a
once-in-a-decade opening

The SHA transition has created urgent, government-mandated demand for compliant digital infrastructure.

🏛️

SHA Mandate (Oct 2024)

Government requires all facilities to submit digital claims via DHA APIs. Non-compliance = no reimbursement.

KHIS Digitization Push

MOH pushing all 13,000+ facilities to submit MOH 705/711/717 electronically. Paper submissions being phased out.

📱

Mobile-First Workforce

Kenya's clinicians are smartphone-native. Starlink + affordable connectivity expanding rapidly into rural areas.

🤖

AI-Ready Moment

LLMs now enable guideline-aligned decision support at scale. First time this is possible for resource-constrained settings.

💰

Incumbent Failure

No existing system handles offline + SHA + KHIS + DPA together. Facilities are actively searching for alternatives.
